Hi GGSO Community,

We hope you are all doing well\! As we get closer to the tournament, we would like to give some friendly reminders and updates. 

**Forms**  
**Every team member** attending the 2027 tournament (including alternates and spectators) must have **three individual forms** on file. These forms should be completed by the team member’s parent or guardian if the team member is under 18\. All PDFs attached are fillable once downloaded. 

* **Form 1: [GGSO Waiver](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14iTRHweKSTBRKyO_ZsBvBo2vg8nDUlQy/view?usp=drive_link)**  
* **Form 2: [RSO Waiver](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1V0ru74kQBXgj-_mU8rby3V9RMBcuoEl-/view?usp=drive_link)**  
* **Form 3: [Authorization to Consent to Treatment](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14LdR96Gzq54Y5GC8YoqQ_0PWG5MeEt9_/view?usp=drive_link)**

**Every participating team** must submit a team roster. 

* **Form 4: [Team Roster](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12cEPEjhgx3nIWpvECAKRTqc7XNBQ8VmS/view?usp=drive_link)**

**Form Submission & Deadline**  
**After collecting all team members’ forms and filling out the team roster, each coach must submit them via [this Google Form](https://forms.gle/xDgD8vx2ivBQZrpB7). The deadline to submit is Friday, January 8th, 2027.** 

Every school (regardless of how many teams) should fill out the Google Form **exactly once**. In the Google Form, you will be asked to select one of two options: 

1. **You may scan all forms and upload them as normal.** Additional instructions for scanning are provided in the Google Form.   
2. **You may scan only the team roster(s) and elect to bring physical copies of the waiver forms to us on tournament day.** Simply check the corresponding option on the Google Form and note that, due to strict university regulations, we will only hand out wristbands according to the number of completed forms we have on file. Please only select this option if you are confident that you won’t forget to bring your forms. 

**Additional Updates**  
The **event list** for the tournament can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fIYqAjl5jb37FH9asgahQzRsMLNXjKq95xSJ_g3K7ck/edit?tab=t.0). This year, we will use **State-level rules** for all events.

**Merchandise**  
Additionally, our **merchandise store is open**\! Check out this year’s designs at the [GGSO Merchandise Store](https://ggsomerchstore.printful.me), featuring hoodies, totes, stickers, and more. We have recently added more designs featuring the GGSO classic logo. **All profits go directly toward supporting the tournament**, so every purchase helps make GGSO possible\!

As always, please feel free to reach out with any questions or concerns\!

Best wishes,  
The GGSO Team
