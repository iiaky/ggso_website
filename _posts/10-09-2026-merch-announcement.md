# 2027 Merchandise Store Open

Hello everyone,

Thank you for filling out our involvement form\! We appreciate your interest and enthusiasm in our tournament. 

We’re excited to announce that our **merchandise store is now open**\! Check out this year’s designs at the [GGSO Merchandise Store](https://ggsomerchstore.printful.me), featuring hoodies, totes, stickers, and more. **All profits go directly toward supporting the tournament**, so every purchase helps make GGSO possible\!

Stay tuned for our subject- and event-specific merchandise line, coming soon\!

As a reminder, all volunteers will receive a **free GGSO t-shirt**, but feel free to grab some additional merch too\!

As always, don’t hesitate to contact us with any questions.
