# 2027 ES/TC Deadline Reminder – Oct. 2nd

Hi GGSO Community,

This is a reminder that the **ES/TC application** for the 11th annual GGSO Invitational at UC Berkeley (hosted on Sunday, January 31st, 2027\) is due on **October 2nd, 2026, at 11:59 PM PT**. The link to the application can be found [here](https://forms.gle/MZfeEZ2QsK7ELTQ48). Please note that these roles are only available to students who have graduated from high school. 

**If you have prior Science Olympiad experience**, consider applying to be an Event Supervisor (ES) or Test Committee (TC) member. Event Supervisors are responsible for writing the tests and supervising competitors on tournament day, while Test Committee members evaluate test drafts prior to tournament day. 

As an added plus, all in-person volunteers will also receive a **free GGSO t-shirt**, guaranteed transportation to and from the UC Berkeley campus on tournament day (for Stanford students), **all-you-can-eat snacks** (while supplies last), and **free lunch**\! Even more reason to get involved and help our tournament be a success.

Thank you all for your continued interest in running our tournament. Don’t hesitate to contact us with any questions\!

Best wishes,   
The GGSO Team
